Our Story

More Than Food,
It's an Experience

Prime Dish was born from a simple but powerful idea: every Nigerian deserves access to delicious, affordable food – delivered fast, fresh, and with love. We started in Lagos in 2023 with just 10 restaurant partners and a dream.

Today, we serve thousands of happy customers across 5 major Nigerian cities, working with over 200 restaurant partners to bring authentic Nigerian and continental cuisine directly to your door.
